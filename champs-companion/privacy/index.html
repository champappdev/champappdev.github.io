Champs Companion — Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Developer: the developer

Champs Companion is a free, unofficial fan-made companion app for Pokémon Champions. It is not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures or The Pokémon Company. This policy explains what data the app handles.

No accounts, no data sent to the developer

The app has no sign-in and does not send your personal information to the developer. The developer does not run any server that collects data from the app.

Data stored only on your device

Your saved teams, custom sets, settings and a cached copy of the game data are stored locally on your device. They are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Game data downloads

To stay current with each regulation, the app downloads public game data from GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub receives standard request information such as your IP address; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Box screenshot scanning

If you choose a screenshot to scan, the text recognition runs on your device and the image is not uploaded. The first time you scan, the app downloads the open-source recognition engine and its English language data from public content delivery networks (cdnjs, operated by Cloudflare, and jsDelivr). Those services receive standard request information such as your IP address.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to serve, measure and (where permitted) personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. Users in regions that require it are asked for consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's advertising policies.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

The floating CHAMPS pill

If you turn on the floating pill, the app uses Android's "Display over other apps" permission only to draw its own panel on top of other apps. It does not read, record or collect anything from other apps or your screen. You can turn it off in the app's Settings or in Android settings at any time.

Links to other sites

The app can open the developer's creator page and donation platforms (such as GitHub Sponsors or Ko-fi) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the creator page.
